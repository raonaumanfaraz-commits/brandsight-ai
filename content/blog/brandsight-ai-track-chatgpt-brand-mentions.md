## The Blind Spot of Modern Growth Teams

Most marketing analytics tools were architected for a world of static search results:
- Google Search Console tracks impressions on traditional Google SERPs.
- SEO rank checkers measure desktop and mobile ranking keywords.
- Web analytics tools track referral traffic after a click has already occurred.

None of them tell you what happens **inside conversational AI models**.

When an AI engine answers a buyer's query, it acts as an authoritative curator. Getting mentioned as the #1 recommended tool can drive hundreds of qualified leads; being omitted means lost market share to competitors who may have worse products but stronger AI citation footprints.
