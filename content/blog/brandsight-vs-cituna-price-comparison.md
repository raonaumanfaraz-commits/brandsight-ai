AI search is now a real traffic channel, and both BrandSight and Cituna answer the same question: when someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation in your category, does your brand show up? Their pricing models work very differently, and that difference matters for your budget. Cituna charges more but checks more engines and writes the fix; BrandSight charges less and tracks more questions.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simple with three flat monthly plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export.

There is no per-response metering.

## Cituna pricing

Cituna is an AI visibility platform that does the work, not only the measuring. It asks your buyers' questions across seven AI engines every day on every plan: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode. For every gap it finds, it writes the fix, and its AutoSEO tool writes articles and publishes them to your CMS.

Every account starts with a 3-day free trial at $0 a month, card required, covering 1 brand with 5 buyer prompts, the top 3 tracked daily. Starter costs $49 a month and covers 1 brand with 10 buyer prompts, daily scans, fix recommendations, 30 AI articles a month, AI crawler analytics, and API and MCP read access. Pro costs $119 a month and covers 3 brands with 30 prompts, daily scans plus alerts, auto-generated fixes, 90 AI articles a month, backlink and SERP-rank data, competitor tracking, and API and MCP write actions. Max costs $399 a month and covers 10 brands with 90 prompts, 300 AI articles a month, and the highest API and MCP allowances. Annual billing charges 10 months for 12. Every plan also includes Google Search Console integration and weekly visibility reports.

Prices as listed on Cituna's pricing page, November 2026.

## Which one fits your budget?

The headline prices are about two and a half times apart: $19 against $49. The real difference is what that money buys. Cituna's 10 prompts sound small next to BrandSight's 50, but each Cituna prompt runs on seven engines instead of three, and the price includes the fix: written recommendations, AI-written articles, and publishing to your CMS. If your problem is getting cited more, not just knowing where you stand, that work is the main reason to pay the higher price.

BrandSight is the cheaper way to track more questions more often: its Pro plan checks 50 prompts daily for $19, roughly 4,500 AI responses a month against about 2,100 on Cituna Starter. Its Agency plan at $49 costs the same as Cituna Starter while covering 20 brands with 200 prompts checked daily. For most small teams, start small and upgrade only when the data proves you need more.
