If you want to know what AI assistants say about your brand, two very different pricing models are on the table. ZipTie charges per check, so your bill grows with every prompt and engine you track. BrandSight charges a flat monthly fee for a fixed number of brands and prompts. The right choice depends on how much you track, so here is the honest math.

## ZipTie pricing

ZipTie prices by the check. One prompt, on one engine, on one check costs $0.01, or $0.0087 a check when you run all seven engines. There are no tiers: you build your own plan from prompt count, refresh frequency, and engine coverage. Seats, projects, and competitors are unlimited on every plan, with no per-brand fee.

The seven engines are ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Bing AI Overview, and Google Gemini. A daily prompt re-runs every 24 hours at no extra cost, so you pay for prompt slots, not for each run. ZipTie's page gives three worked monthly examples, before add-ons. An in-house brand running 50 daily, 300 weekly, and 1,000 monthly prompts across all seven engines costs $230.95 a month, or $184.76 a month paid yearly. An agency with ten clients at 50 weekly prompts each costs $130.72 a month, or $104.58 a month yearly. An enterprise setup with 1,000 monthly prompts costs $61.00 a month, or $48.80 a month yearly. Longer billing periods discount the prompt subtotal: 3 months saves 5%, 6 months saves 10%, and annual saves 20%.

Optional add-ons cost extra: UGC Impact Analysis adds 80% to the bill, Content Generation is $20 a month per context, and API and MCP access is $10 a month. The free trial runs 7 days with 25 daily prompts across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode, with no card required.

Prices as listed on ZipTie pricing page, November 2026.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it flat. The Free plan costs $0 a month and tracks 1 brand with 5 prompts on weekly checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini. The Pro plan costs $19 a month and tracks 5 brands with 50 prompts on daily checks, adding DeepSeek to the engines. The Agency plan costs $49 a month and tracks 20 brands with 200 prompts on daily checks, with white-label executive reports and CSV and JSON API export.

There are no per-check charges, no add-ons to buy, and no usage meter running in the background. What you see on the pricing page is what you pay.

## Side by side

The gap is widest for small setups. A solo founder tracking one brand with 50 prompts pays $19 a month on BrandSight Pro. On ZipTie, even the lightest worked example, 1,000 monthly prompts across seven engines, is $61 a month. For a small agency, BrandSight Agency at $49 a month covers 20 brands and 200 daily prompts, while ZipTie's agency example with ten clients is $130.72 a month.

ZipTie's model makes more sense at the extremes. If you track a handful of prompts on one engine, a few dollars of checks could cost less than a flat plan. And if you need all seven engines, including Perplexity and Copilot, ZipTie covers engines BrandSight does not. ZipTie also charges nothing per seat, project, or competitor, so a large team pays the same as a small one for the same prompt volume.

BrandSight wins on predictability. The bill never moves with your usage, and the Pro and Agency plans include competitor share-of-voice alerts and daily checks without add-ons. ZipTie wins on flexibility and engine coverage, if you are comfortable with a bill that changes as your tracking grows.
