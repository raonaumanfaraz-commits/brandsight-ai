Ask an AI assistant about your brand and it will answer with confidence, even when the facts are wrong. It may quote an old price, list a feature you retired, describe a competitor's product as yours, or invent a detail that never existed. Buyers treat that answer as true, so a wrong answer is not a curiosity, it is lost business. Here is how to fix it.

## Step 1: Capture the wrong answer exactly

Run the prompts that matter, in a signed-out browser or a fresh chat, and screenshot every wrong answer in full. Write down the exact wrong claim, word for word, and the date you saw it. AI answers change daily, so without a screenshot you cannot prove the fix worked later. Check the same prompt in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity separately, because each one can be wrong in a different way, and fixing one does not fix the others.

## Step 2: Find the source behind the error

Most AI answers cite their sources, and the wrong fact usually comes from a real page: an old pricing page, a directory listing, a review site, or a press article that was never updated. Open every citation and look for the wrong claim. If the answer has no citations, search the exact wrong phrase on Google; the page that ranks for it is usually the source the model read. Keep a short list: wrong claim, source page, and who controls that page.

## Step 3: Fix the source page first

You cannot edit an AI model, but you can fix the pages it reads. If the wrong fact sits on your own site, correct it there and make the right fact easy to quote: one short sentence with the current price, the current feature list, or the current fact, near the top of the page. If it sits on a directory or review site, claim the listing and update it, or ask the site owner to correct it. If it sits in an old blog post or news article, publish a clear update on your own site that states the correct fact, so the model has a newer, clearer source to prefer.

## Step 4: Publish a facts page the models can trust

Create one page on your site that states the facts about your brand in plain sentences: what you do, your current prices, your current features, and who you serve. Avoid marketing language and vague claims; models quote pages that state facts directly. Link to this page from your homepage and your pricing page, and keep it updated whenever a price or feature changes. Over weeks, this page becomes the source the models cite most, because it is the clearest one available.

## Step 5: Re-test on a fixed schedule

Re-run the same prompts once a week and record whether each wrong claim is still there. Most corrections take two to four weeks to show up, because models refresh their sources on their own schedule, not yours. Do not rewrite the source page every day; one clear correction, then patience, then a re-test. If a claim is still wrong after a month, the source is probably a page you have not found yet, so search again with different wording and check the citations once more.
