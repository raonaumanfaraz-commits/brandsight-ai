Meta AI is Meta's assistant, built on its Llama models and living inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and Messenger, plus a standalone app and website at meta.ai. People meet it inside apps they already open every day. When someone asks it for the best tool, agency, or product in your category, your brand either appears or it does not. Meta AI has no brand dashboard and no way to submit your brand for a mention, so tracking visibility there is a manual routine. Here is a method that takes about thirty minutes a week.

## Step 1: Fix your prompt list

Pick ten to fifteen questions your buyers actually ask, mixing three kinds: direct comparisons ("best AI visibility tool for small agencies"), problem questions ("how do I track brand mentions in AI answers"), and category questions ("AI search analytics tools for SEO teams"). Write them down exactly as a buyer would type them, and keep the wording fixed week to week, or you cannot compare weeks.

Include your brand name in only two or three prompts; the rest should be brand-free, because that is how buyers search before they know you exist.

## Step 2: Ask Meta AI the same way every week

Once a week, run the full list in one place, a dedicated Meta AI thread in WhatsApp or the standalone app at meta.ai, and use that same entry point every week. Meta AI has memory across conversations and apps, so use the same account each time and start a fresh chat, because personalisation changes answers. Do not click any result while you work through the list.

For each prompt, record in a sheet whether your brand was mentioned, where in the answer it appeared, which sources Meta AI cited, and whether a competitor was recommended instead. Mention rate, position, and citations are the three numbers that matter.

## Step 3: Read the sources, not just the mention

A mention without a source is weak visibility. Meta AI grounds its answers in real-time web results and public content from Meta's own apps, so when it links a source, open it and check two things: is the page yours, and does it actually answer the question? If it cites a competitor's page for your category, that page is the one to beat; if it cites a review roundup that leaves you out, getting listed there is the fix.

Also note when Meta AI's answer is wrong or outdated about your brand, and fix it at the source page, because Meta AI will keep repeating what its sources say.

## Step 4: Keep your Meta business profiles in order

Because the assistant lives inside Meta's apps, the business information on your Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp Business profiles is close at hand: description, hours, location, and links. Keep all three accurate and consistent; an assistant repeats inconsistent details back as fact.

On your own website, check that Meta's crawler is allowed in: Meta AI's web grounding relies on its own crawler, Meta-ExternalAgent, which you can allow or block in robots.txt. Blocked, and your pages cannot be used as sources.

## Step 5: Compare week to week

After four weeks you have a trend, not a snapshot. Look for three patterns: prompts where you never appear, prompts where you appear below a competitor, and prompts where you appeared and then vanished. The first group needs new content, the second needs stronger pages, and the third usually means a source page changed or a competitor published better.

Do not react to a single week: AI answers vary, so only a change that holds for two weeks in a row is worth acting on.
