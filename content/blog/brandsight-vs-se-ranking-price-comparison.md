If you want to know how AI search engines talk about your brand, two very different tools can do the job. SE Ranking is a full SEO platform that now includes AI visibility tracking, while BrandSight is a dedicated AI visibility tracker. The price gap between them is large, so it is worth understanding what each dollar actually buys.

## SE Ranking pricing

SE Ranking sells its platform in three tiers. Core costs $129 a month, or $103.20 a month billed annually, and includes 10 projects, 1 manager seat, and 2,000 keywords tracked daily. Growth costs $279 a month, or $223.20 a month billed annually, and includes 30 projects, 3 manager seats, and 5,000 keywords tracked daily. Enterprise is custom priced.

Both Core and Growth include AI Visibility tracking: 25 prompts a day across 5 AI engines, with daily tracking. SE Ranking also sells AI visibility as a standalone add-on from $71 a month, with tiers from 75 to 1,000 prompts. A free 14-day trial is available, and it includes 20 AI prompts a day.

Prices as listed on SE Ranking pricing page, November 2026.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight is built for one job: tracking how AI search engines mention, rank, and cite your brand. Its Free plan costs $0 and covers 1 brand with 5 prompts and weekly checks. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 prompts and daily checks. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 prompts, daily checks, and white-label reports.

BrandSight tracks mentions across ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek, with competitor share-of-voice alerts on paid plans. Prices verified live on brandsight-ai.coderao.com/pricing, November 2026.

## Price per prompt

This is where the difference is starkest. On SE Ranking Core at $129 a month, the included AI visibility allowance is 25 prompts a day. On BrandSight Pro at $19 a month, you get 50 prompts with daily checks. BrandSight Agency at $49 a month gives you 200 prompts, still less than half the price of SE Ranking Core.

SE Ranking's standalone AI visibility add-on starts at $71 a month, which is still more than BrandSight Agency. If AI visibility is the only thing you need, the dedicated tool costs far less.

## What SE Ranking adds for the extra money

The higher price is not wasted money, it buys a different product. SE Ranking Core includes rank tracking, keyword and backlink research, site audits, content marketing tools, and local marketing features. If your team already needs a full SEO suite, the AI visibility feature comes along for the ride, and paying for a second tool may be unnecessary.

BrandSight does not do rank tracking, backlink analysis, or site audits. It does one thing: AI visibility, with prompts, mentions, citations, and competitor tracking, at a price a small team or solo founder can justify for that single job.

## Which one should you choose?

Choose SE Ranking if you need a complete SEO platform and AI visibility is one feature among many. Its Core plan makes sense when rank tracking, audits, and keyword research are already on your shopping list.

Choose BrandSight if AI visibility is the job. A freelancer, a SaaS startup, or a small agency that only needs to know how ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek describe its brands gets daily tracking, more prompts, and a lower bill, with a free plan to start.

The cheapest way to decide is to test both on your own brand. Run the free BrandSight check at [BrandSight AI](https://brandsight-ai.coderao.com) and compare it against what your current SEO tool tells you about AI search. The answer that mentions your brand more accurately is the one worth paying for.
