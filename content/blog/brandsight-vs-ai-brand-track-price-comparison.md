AI search is now a real traffic channel, and both BrandSight and AI Brand Track exist to answer the same question: when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation in your category, does your brand show up? The tools are similar in purpose, but their pricing models work very differently, and that difference matters for your budget.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simple with three flat monthly plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export.

There is no per-prompt metering and no usage-based billing on any plan. What you see is what you pay, and the free tier is genuinely free, not a time-limited trial.

## AI Brand Track pricing

AI Brand Track charges by subscription and by credits. Its Starter plan costs $39 a month, after a 14-day test that costs $9, and covers 1 brand with weekly checks, fixes written for you, and 53 credits a month. The Pro plan costs $69 a month and covers unlimited brands with weekly checks, fixes written for you, 154 credits a month, and keyword data from DataForSEO. An agency plan with white-label reports is listed as coming soon, with no price yet.

If you prefer to pay once instead of subscribing, AI Brand Track sells credits with no expiry. A Single Analysis costs $49 for 53 credits, about four full checks, and a Credit Pack costs $149 for 220 credits, about twenty full checks. Smaller top-ups are also available: 10 credits for $12, 25 credits for $25, and 50 credits for $45. Every price is public, with no sales call needed.

## Which one fits your budget?

The biggest difference is the check frequency. BrandSight runs daily auto-checks on every paid plan, while AI Brand Track runs weekly checks on both of its subscription plans. If you publish often or your competitors move fast, daily tracking catches changes sooner. Weekly checks are fine if you only need a regular snapshot.

The second difference is how the price scales. BrandSight charges a flat monthly rate for a fixed number of brands and prompts, so the cost is the same every month. AI Brand Track ties its usage to credits, so heavy checking can cost extra, although its one-off credit packs let you pay only for what you use, with no expiry on the credits.

On raw price, BrandSight Pro at $19 a month is half the cost of AI Brand Track Starter at $39 a month, and it covers five brands instead of one, with daily checks instead of weekly.

## The bottom line

Both tools track your brand across AI search, and both publish their prices openly. If you want flat monthly pricing, daily checks, and a genuinely free tier to start with, BrandSight is the cheaper and simpler option. If you prefer to pay once for credits that never expire, AI Brand Track gives you that flexibility.
