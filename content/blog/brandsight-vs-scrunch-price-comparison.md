Scrunch and BrandSight AI both track how your brand appears in AI search answers, but they are priced for very different buyers. Scrunch is a mid-market platform that pairs visibility tracking with site audits and an agent experience platform that serves AI-friendly versions of your pages. BrandSight is a focused tracker that checks whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek mention your brand, where you appear in the answer, and which sources get cited. This comparison uses the prices listed on each company's own pricing page in November 2026.

## Scrunch prices

Prices as listed on Scrunch pricing page, November 2026. Scrunch Core costs $250 a month and includes 125 unique prompts, 5,000 responses a month, 5 site audits a month, 1 brand workspace, and 5 user licenses. It tracks 4 AI models: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot. It covers 5 competitors, 1 country, 1 language, and 1 persona, and includes sentiment analysis, citation tracking, and insights and recommendations. Scrunch Enterprise is custom priced and expands coverage to 9 AI models, adding Claude, Gemini, Meta AI, Google AI Mode, and Grok, plus the Agent Experience Platform, API access, SSO, and a dedicated account team.

## BrandSight prices

BrandSight's prices, confirmed on its own pricing page in November 2026, are Free at $0 a month, Pro at $19 a month, and Agency at $49 a month. The free tier tracks 1 brand with 5 prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro tracks 5 brands with 50 prompts and daily auto-checks across OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek, and adds competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency tracks 20 brands with 200 prompts daily and adds white-label executive reports and CSV and JSON export.

## What the price gap buys

Scrunch Core costs about thirteen times BrandSight Pro, and the extra money buys scale and depth. Core alone includes 125 prompts against BrandSight Pro's 50, five site audits a month with page optimization suggestions, and tracking of AI bot traffic on your site. Enterprise adds the Agent Experience Platform, which serves machine-readable versions of your pages to AI agents, plus API access and Looker Studio reporting. Scrunch also tracks five competitors on its entry plan, while BrandSight's competitor alerts are a Pro feature rather than a separate dashboard. BrandSight does not do site audits, page optimizations, or bot traffic analysis. It runs your prompts through the AI models and reports mention rate, position, and citations.

The engine lists also differ. Scrunch Core covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Copilot, with Gemini and Claude held for the custom Enterprise plan. BrandSight Pro includes OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek in the $19 price, and its free tier already includes OpenAI and Gemini. If Gemini coverage matters to you, it is included at BrandSight's lowest paid tier and reserved for Scrunch's enterprise tier.

## Which one fits your budget

If you are a mid-market team or an agency that wants site audits, bot traffic data, and an agent experience platform alongside visibility tracking, Scrunch Core is built for that job, and its $250 a month price reflects it. If you are a solo founder, a small agency, or a SaaS startup that only needs to know whether AI assistants mention your brand and how often, BrandSight Pro covers the core job for $19 a month, and the free tier lets you check your visibility before paying anything.
