If you want to know what AI assistants say about your brand, Evertune and BrandSight take two very different approaches to pricing. Evertune sells prompt credits and bundles in an ads agent, with prices that climb fast. BrandSight sells flat monthly plans with a fixed number of brands and prompts. Here is what each one costs, and which one fits your budget.

## Evertune pricing

Evertune has three plans. The Starter plan costs $60 a month and includes 5,000 prompt credits a month, AI visibility tracking on up to 5 models, an agent that analyses your data and gives action steps, and the ability to run and optimise ChatGPT ads. The first 7 days are free, and the Ads Agent takes 10% of media spend.

The Pro plan costs $800 a month, billed annually, and includes 100,000 prompt credits a month, AI visibility tracking on all major models, a Content Agent that generates and optimises content, prompt research tools, AI retargeting, and onboarding support. The Ads Agent fee drops to 8% of media spend on this plan.

The Enterprise plan is custom priced, with custom prompt credits, a custom Ads Agent fee, dedicated customer support, unlimited content generation, a Fact Checker, AI website optimisation, data exports, and SSO integration.

Evertune also sells by prompt credits. Its page includes a calculator that turns a prompt count into a monthly credit need, and a 50-prompt setup works out at 1,500 prompt credits a month. Daily monitoring, competitor benchmarking, ChatGPT Shopping Insights, and the GEO Manager Agent are included on all three plans.

Prices as listed on Evertune pricing page, November 2026.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it flat. The Free plan costs $0 a month and tracks 1 brand with 5 prompts on weekly checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini. The Pro plan costs $19 a month and tracks 5 brands with 50 prompts on daily checks, adding DeepSeek to the engines. The Agency plan costs $49 a month and tracks 20 brands with 200 prompts on daily checks, with white-label executive reports and CSV and JSON API export.

There are no prompt credits to count, no media percentage to pay, and no add-ons to buy. What you see on the pricing page is what you pay.

## Side by side

The gap is stark for small setups. A solo founder tracking one brand with 50 prompts pays $19 a month on BrandSight Pro. On Evertune, the Starter plan is $60 a month for 5,000 prompt credits, and the plan is built around running ChatGPT ads, which is a different job from simply knowing where your brand stands.

For agencies, the gap grows. BrandSight Agency at $49 a month covers 20 brands and 200 daily prompts with white-label reports. Evertune Pro at $800 a month is aimed at teams that also want a Content Agent, AI retargeting, and managed ad spend, so the price buys a much bigger service, not just tracking.

Evertune wins on two points. It tracks up to 11 models on Pro and Enterprise, against BrandSight's three, and it pairs visibility data with an ads agent that can act on the results inside ChatGPT. If you are already spending on AI search ads, that combination may justify the higher price.

## Which should you choose?

Choose BrandSight if you want a fixed monthly cost and daily checks without counting credits or paying a share of ad spend. Choose Evertune if you need 11-model coverage and want the same platform to run ChatGPT ads for you.
