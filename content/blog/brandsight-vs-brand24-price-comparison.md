Brand monitoring prices vary enormously, and Brand24 is a good example of the upper end. Its Individual plan lists $249 a month, while BrandSight Pro costs $19 a month. Both tools track brands, but they are built for different jobs, and the price gap reflects that. This comparison sticks to the published prices and the plan details shown on each company's own pricing page.

## The prices, side by side

Brand24 lists five plans on its pricing page. Individual costs $249 a month, or $199 a month billed annually. Team costs $349 a month, or $299 billed annually. Pro costs $499 a month, or $399 billed annually. Business costs $699 a month, or $599 billed annually. Enterprise starts from $1,499 a month, billed annually. Brand24 also offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

BrandSight's pricing is simpler. The Free tier costs $0 a month and tracks 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks. Pro costs $19 a month and tracks 5 brands with 50 search prompts and daily auto-checks. The Agency plan costs $49 a month and tracks 20 brands with 200 search prompts and daily auto-checks.

Prices as listed on Brand24 pricing page, October 2026. BrandSight prices as listed on brandsight-ai.coderao.com, October 2026.

## What each tool is built to track

Brand24 is a broad media monitoring tool. It tracks mentions across social platforms, news, blogs, forums, and review sites, with analytics for reach, engagement, sentiment, and influencer analysis. Its AI Visibility module, which tracks a brand's exposure across AI platforms, is listed as an add-on on every Brand24 plan, including Individual, rather than part of the base plan price.

BrandSight is narrower by design. It tracks what AI search engines say about a brand: mentions, position, and citations across ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek, plus competitor share-of-voice alerts. It does not try to cover every social channel. For a team whose main question is "what do AI answers say about us," that focus is the whole product, and it is included in the $19 Pro plan rather than sold as an add-on.

## Doing the annual maths

The monthly gap compounds quickly. A year of BrandSight Pro at $19 a month costs $228. A year of Brand24 Individual at $249 a month costs $2,988, or $2,388 on the $199 annual rate. That is a difference of more than $2,100 a year at the annual rate, and more on monthly billing.

That does not make Brand24 overpriced. A larger team that needs broad media monitoring, social listening, and reporting across many channels may use far more of Brand24 than a small team ever would. But a small agency, a startup, or a freelancer tracking a handful of brands in AI search is paying for coverage they may never open. Price should follow the job, not the brand name on the invoice.

## Which one fits your budget

Choose Brand24 Individual if you need broad monitoring across social, news, and review sources, with reporting and analytics, and your budget supports $249 a month. Expect to ask about the AI Visibility add-on separately if AI search tracking is part of your brief.

Choose BrandSight Pro if your priority is AI search visibility itself: mentions, position, and citations in ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek answers, checked daily, for up to 5 brands at $19 a month. Start on the free tier if you only track one brand, and step up to the $49 Agency plan when you manage client brands at scale.
