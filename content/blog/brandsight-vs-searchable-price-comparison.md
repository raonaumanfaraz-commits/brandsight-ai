AI search is now a real traffic channel, and both BrandSight and Searchable answer the same question: when someone asks ChatGPT or another AI assistant for a recommendation in your category, does your brand show up? Their pricing models work very differently. Searchable is a full AI search visibility platform that bundles page audits and article generation with tracking; BrandSight is a focused AI visibility tracker.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simple with three flat monthly plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export. There is no per-response metering and no charge per seat.

## Searchable pricing

Searchable charges by prompt volume per model, with separate plans for in-house brands and for agencies. Every plan includes ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity, tracked daily. Other models are paid add-ons, priced per plan.

Pro costs $125 a month, or $100 a month billed annually, and covers 100 tracked prompts per model, 200 site audits a month, 20 articles a month, and 1 project, with reports, MCP, and API access. It includes a 14-day free trial. Scale costs $400 a month, or $320 a month billed annually, and covers 500 tracked prompts per model, 1,000 audits a month, 80 articles a month, and 10 projects, adding Looker Studio and white-label reports. Enterprise costs $999 or more a month and covers 1,250 tracked prompts per model and unlimited projects, with a dedicated AEO specialist. A Custom plan sits above Enterprise with no published price.

Model add-ons on the Pro plan cost $25 a month each for Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, and Google AI Mode, $50 a month each for Claude Lite, Gemini, and Grok Lite, and $200 a month each for full Claude, Grok, and Meta Muse. A Pro plan tracking Gemini as well as the three included engines costs $175 a month. Agency plans start at $250 a month, with pitch prompts, white-label reports, and Looker Studio on every tier.

Prices as listed on Searchable's pricing page, November 2026.

## What the price gap buys

The gap between $19 and $125 a month is mostly about what sits around the tracking. Searchable bundles site audits, AI-written articles, MCP access, and API access into every brand plan, and its Scale plan adds Looker Studio and white-label reporting. If you want one platform that audits pages and drafts the articles to fix them, that bundle is the point.

BrandSight takes the opposite approach: tracking only, at a flat price, with no add-on charges for engines. On Pro, Gemini and DeepSeek are included, so the price you see is the price you pay for all three engines.

## Which one fits

Pick Searchable if you run a content operation that needs audits and articles in the same subscription, and the $125 starting price fits your budget. The 14-day trial is the way to test whether the article and audit features earn their keep.

Pick BrandSight if you want to know where your brand stands in AI answers without paying for a content suite around it. The free tier covers a single brand with weekly checks, and Pro at $19 a month covers five brands with daily checks across ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek. For an agency watching many client brands, the Agency plan at $49 a month covers 20 brands with 200 prompts and white-label reports, which is less than a single Searchable Pro plan.
