TrendlyAI and BrandSight both answer the same question: what do AI assistants say about your brand? Both start at $19 a month, which makes the comparison unusually direct. The difference is not the price on the first plan. It is how often you get checked, how many brands and prompts you get, and which engines are covered.

## TrendlyAI pricing

TrendlyAI tracks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews, and every plan scans weekly. Prices as listed on TrendlyAI pricing page, November 2026.

The Solo plan costs $19 a month, or $16 a month billed yearly at $190 a year. It covers 1 brand with 25 prompts, weekly scans on all four engines, tracking against 2 competitors, 10 fix drafts a month, white-label PDF reports, and 5 pitch audits a month.

The Pro plan costs $49 a month, or $39 a month billed yearly at $468 a year. It covers 3 brands with 25 prompts each, 5 competitors per brand, unlimited fix drafts, and 10 pitch audits a month, with extra brands at $15 a month each.

The Agency plan costs $149 a month, or $124 a month billed yearly at $1,490 a year. It covers 10 client brands with 25 prompts each, 25 pitch audits a month, and priority support. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and no card is needed to start it.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight tracks OpenAI, Google Gemini and DeepSeek, and every paid plan checks daily. Prices verified live on the BrandSight pricing page, November 2026.

The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 1 brand, 5 search prompts, weekly auto-checks, and tracking on OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report.

The Pro plan costs $19 a month and includes 5 brands, 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, DeepSeek alongside OpenAI and Gemini, and competitor share-of-voice alerts.

The Agency plan costs $49 a month and includes 20 brands, 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, client CSV and JSON API export, and dedicated priority support.

## The real difference: frequency vs fix drafts

At the $19 entry point, BrandSight gives you more of the basics: five brands instead of one, fifty prompts instead of twenty-five, and daily checks instead of weekly. TrendlyAI covers one more engine, Claude, plus Perplexity, and adds two things BrandSight does not list: fix drafts that turn gaps into content suggestions, and pitch audits you can send to prospects before a sales call.

That trade-off matters most for timing. AI answers change between runs, so a weekly scan shows a trend with a one-week delay, while a daily check catches a lost citation or a new competitor mention the same week it happens. If you are watching a launch, a campaign, or a competitor's sudden visibility, daily checks are the practical difference.

At agency scale the gap widens. TrendlyAI Agency costs $149 a month for 10 client brands, while BrandSight Agency costs $49 a month for 20 brands, both with white-label reports. TrendlyAI adds more pitch audits and priority support at that tier, but BrandSight covers twice the brands at a third of the price.

## Which one fits your budget?

If you want weekly tracking with built-in fix drafts and pitch audits for prospecting, and your buyers mostly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude, TrendlyAI Solo at $19 a month is a fair deal for a single brand.

If you want daily tracking, more brands and more prompts at the same $19, and your buyers ask Gemini and DeepSeek as well, BrandSight Pro covers five brands for the price TrendlyAI charges for one.
