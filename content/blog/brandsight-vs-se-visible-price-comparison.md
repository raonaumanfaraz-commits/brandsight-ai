SE Visible and BrandSight both track what AI search engines say about your brand, but they sit at very different price points. SE Visible is SE Ranking's standalone AI visibility platform, built for teams that want deep daily data across several engines. BrandSight is a leaner tracker aimed at founders, creators and small agencies. The right choice depends on how many prompts you need, how many brands you manage and how much you want to spend.

## SE Visible pricing

SE Visible sells three self-serve plans plus a custom Enterprise tier. Prices as listed on SE Visible pricing page, November 2026.

Basic costs $89 a month, or $71 a month billed annually. It includes 75 tracked prompts, around 11,250 AI answers a month, 5 projects, one location per project and daily tracking. Core costs $189 a month, or $151 a month billed annually, for 200 prompts, around 30,000 answers a month, 10 projects and unlimited locations. Agency costs $399 a month, or $319 a month billed annually, for 1,000 prompts, around 180,000 answers a month, 30 projects, daily or weekly tracking, Claude tracking and a customer success manager. Enterprise is custom priced from 2,000 prompts. Every plan includes unlimited user seats, unlimited competitors, API access, MCP access and a Data Studio connector, and a 14-day free trial is offered.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps its pricing simple, with three flat tiers. Prices verified live on brandsight-ai.coderao.com/pricing, November 2026.

The Free plan costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand, 5 search prompts and weekly checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands, 50 search prompts and daily checks across OpenAI, Gemini and DeepSeek, plus competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands, 200 search prompts, daily checks, white-label executive reports and API export.

## What you get for the money

The biggest difference is prompt volume per dollar. SE Visible's Basic plan gives you 75 prompts a day for $89 a month, and each prompt is checked in every engine on the plan at no extra cost. BrandSight Pro gives you 50 prompts a day for $19 a month. If you track a large prompt library across several markets, SE Visible's higher volume makes sense. If you track a focused set of prompts for one brand or a handful of clients, BrandSight covers the same daily job for a fifth of the price.

Engine coverage also differs. SE Visible checks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot, with Claude available on the Agency plan. BrandSight checks OpenAI, Gemini and DeepSeek. Perplexity is not on SE Visible's pricing page engine list, so if Perplexity answers matter to your audience, neither tool's listed engine set is a perfect fit on its own.

Both tools report brand mentions, share of voice, average position, sentiment and cited sources. SE Visible adds deeper citation analytics, including cited domains and pages, fan-out queries and a Data Studio connector on every plan. BrandSight focuses on the essentials: daily mention and citation tracking, competitor benchmarking and simple reports that a small team can read in minutes.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose SE Visible if you manage several brands across multiple locations, need hundreds of prompts checked daily and want the reporting depth of an enterprise SEO family. Its Basic plan is a serious commitment, but the per-prompt cost falls fast as you move up the tiers.

Choose BrandSight if you are a founder, creator or small agency watching one to twenty brands and you want daily AI visibility tracking without a three-figure monthly bill. The free plan lets you test the data before you pay anything, and Pro at $19 a month covers most small-team needs.

Price alone rarely decides a tool, but here the gap is hard to ignore: SE Visible's entry plan costs more than four times BrandSight Pro. Start with the tool that matches the size of your prompt list today, and upgrade when your tracking needs outgrow it.
