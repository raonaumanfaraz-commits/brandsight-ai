Both will tell you what ChatGPT and Gemini say about your brand, but the bill looks very different.

## Rankscale pricing

Rankscale sells credits, not prompts. Each plan includes a monthly credit allowance, and the number of AI answers you get depends on the plan. Prices as listed on Rankscale pricing page, November 2026.

The Essentials plan costs $17 a month, but only when billed yearly. It includes 120 credits a month, 2 brand dashboards, up to 480 answers from AI engines, 10 page audits a month, and tracking on 17+ engines with scheduling from hourly to monthly.

The Pro plan costs $99 a month, or $84 a month billed yearly. It includes 1,200 credits a month, 10 brand dashboards, up to 4,800 answers, and 50 page audits a month.

Pro+ costs $229 a month ($195 billed yearly) with 3,000 credits, 25 dashboards, up to 12,000 answers, and 100 audits. Growth costs $385 a month ($327 yearly) with 5,500 credits, 50 dashboards, up to 22,000 answers, and 200 audits. Enterprise costs $780 a month ($663 yearly) with 12,000 credits, 100 dashboards, up to 48,000 answers, and 200 audits.

Every plan includes unlimited search terms, all regions, competitor benchmarking, citations analysis, sentiment analysis, custom dashboards, shareable dashboard links, and CSV and Sheets exports. Credit rollover of up to 2x applies on Pro and Pro+, and up to 3x on Growth and Enterprise, with top-ups anytime in the app. White-labelled dashboard links and REST API access arrive on the higher plans.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight charges a flat monthly rate for a fixed number of brands and prompts, verified live on the BrandSight pricing page, November 2026.

The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 1 brand, 5 search prompts, weekly auto-checks, and tracking on OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report.

The Pro plan costs $19 a month and includes 5 brands, 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, DeepSeek alongside OpenAI and Gemini, and competitor share-of-voice alerts.

The Agency plan costs $49 a month and includes 20 brands, 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, client CSV and JSON API export, and dedicated priority support.

## The real difference: credits vs flat prompts

Rankscale's strength is breadth: 17-plus engines, hourly scheduling, and deep analytics suit teams tracking many brands across many engines. The trade-off is predictability: how far your credits stretch depends on how many engines you run and how often, so the monthly cost moves with your usage.

BrandSight's strength is simplicity. You pay one flat price, you get a fixed number of prompts, and daily checks are included on every paid plan. There is nothing to calculate per engine and no credit balance to watch. The trade-off is scope: fewer engines and a smaller prompt allowance than Rankscale's larger plans.

## Which one fits your budget?

If you are a small brand or an agency watching a handful of brands with a fixed set of questions, BrandSight's Pro plan at $19 a month covers daily tracking for less than Rankscale's entry plan, and the Agency plan at $49 a month still costs half of Rankscale Pro.

If you are a larger team tracking dozens of brands across many engines, refreshing hourly, and building custom dashboards, Rankscale's credit model and engine coverage may justify the higher spend, starting at $99 a month on Pro for monthly billing.
