Rank Prompt and BrandSight both track what AI assistants say about your brand, but they charge for it in completely different ways. Rank Prompt sells monthly credits you spend across seven engines, while BrandSight sells flat plans with a fixed number of prompts and brands. The entry prices look close, but the cost of one tracked prompt is not.

## Rank Prompt pricing

Rank Prompt tracks seven engines on every plan: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Muse. Prices as listed on Rank Prompt pricing page, November 2026.

The Free plan costs $0 and includes 10 credits a month, enough for one full visibility report, with no card required. Every paid plan comes with a 7-day free trial and 50 bonus credits.

The Starter plan costs $49 a month, or $39 a month billed yearly. It includes 150 credits a month, 1 brand, 10 locations per brand, and a $5 monthly AI agent budget. The Pro plan costs $89 a month, or $71 a month billed yearly, with 500 credits, 10 brands, 2 team seats, and a $10 agent budget. The Agency plan costs $149 a month, or $119 a month billed yearly, with 1,000 credits, 50 brands, 10 seats, white-label branded reports and a client portal link per brand. The Agency Plus plan costs $299 a month, or $239 a month billed yearly, with 2,000 credits, 500 brands, 25 seats, and the whole app white-labelled on your own domain.

Credits are the part to read carefully. One prompt costs 1 credit across up to three engines, 2 credits across four to six engines, and 3 credits across all seven. An AI article costs 10 credits, a brand audit costs 10, and credits reset monthly and do not roll over.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight tracks OpenAI, Google Gemini and DeepSeek, and every paid plan checks daily. Prices verified live on the BrandSight pricing page, November 2026.

The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 1 brand, 5 search prompts, weekly auto-checks, and tracking on OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report.

The Pro plan costs $19 a month and includes 5 brands, 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, DeepSeek alongside OpenAI and Gemini, and competitor share-of-voice alerts.

The Agency plan costs $49 a month and includes 20 brands, 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, client CSV and JSON API export, and dedicated priority support.

## The real difference: credits vs flat prompts

At the entry level, Rank Prompt Starter costs $49 a month for 150 credits, while BrandSight Pro costs $19 a month for 50 prompts. Rank Prompt gives you more runs per dollar if you track on only one to three engines, because 150 credits buy 150 prompt runs that way. But run the same prompt across all seven engines and each run costs 3 credits, so those 150 credits cover only 50 full-coverage prompts, the same count BrandSight Pro gives for $19.

Rank Prompt also covers more ground: seven engines against three, plus Claude, Grok and Muse, and it bundles content tools BrandSight does not list, like AI article generation, a hosted blog, and citation outreach. If you want one platform that tracks visibility and then writes the fixes, Rank Prompt is the broader tool.

BrandSight is the simpler and cheaper buy for tracking alone. It costs $19 a month for five brands with daily checks, while Rank Prompt charges $49 for one brand at the same daily cadence, and $89 for ten. At agency scale the gap is wider still: BrandSight Agency covers 20 brands for $49 a month, while Rank Prompt Agency covers 50 brands for $149.

## Which one fits your budget?

If you need seven engines, credit-based control of your spend, and built-in content and outreach tools, Rank Prompt Starter at $49 a month is a fair deal, especially on the $39 annual rate.

If you want daily tracking of several brands at a flat price, with no credit maths and no unused-credit waste at month end, BrandSight Pro covers five brands for $19 a month. Try BrandSight free at https://brandsight-ai.coderao.com and see what AI assistants say about your brand today.
