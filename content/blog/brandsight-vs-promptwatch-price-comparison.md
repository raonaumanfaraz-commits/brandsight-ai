AI search is now a real traffic channel, and both BrandSight and Promptwatch exist to answer the same question: when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation in your category, does your brand show up? The tools are similar in purpose, but their prices sit in very different leagues.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simple with three flat monthly plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export.

There is no per-prompt metering and no usage-based billing on any plan. What you see is what you pay, and the free tier is genuinely free, not a time-limited trial.

## Promptwatch pricing

Promptwatch is the bigger, heavier platform of the two, and its prices reflect that. The Essential plan costs $95 a month and covers 1 project with unlimited seats, 50 prompts, 6,000 responses, daily tracking, country-level tracking, agent chat, and MCP and API access. The Professional plan costs $245 a month and covers 2 projects with 150 prompts, 18,000 responses, content writing and refresh, a knowledge base, and agent analytics. The Business plan costs $579 a month and covers 5 projects with 350 prompts, 42,000 responses, up to hourly tracking, state and city level tracking, shopping insights, ads radar, and fact checking. Enterprise is custom priced, with SSO, white labelling, a dedicated success manager, and priority support.

Prices as listed on Promptwatch pricing page, November 2026.

## Side by side

| | BrandSight | Promptwatch |
|---|---|---|
| Entry paid plan | Pro, $19/mo | Essential, $95/mo |
| Prompts on entry plan | 50 | 50 |
| Brands/projects on entry plan | 5 brands | 1 project |
| Tracking frequency | Daily on paid plans | Daily on all paid plans |
| Mid plan | Agency, $49/mo | Professional, $245/mo |
| Free option | Free tier, $0/mo | None listed on the pricing page |

The most striking number is the entry point. Promptwatch Essential costs five times BrandSight Pro while offering the same 50 prompts, and it covers one project where BrandSight Pro covers five brands. Promptwatch justifies the premium with its content agents, knowledge base, hourly tracking on Business, and a much wider model list. If you need a platform that also writes and refreshes your content, that extra capability may be worth paying for.

## Who should pick which

Choose BrandSight if you are a solo founder, a small SaaS team, or an agency watching a handful of client brands and you want daily AI visibility tracking at a flat, predictable price. At $19 a month, Pro gives you the same prompt count as Promptwatch Essential for a fifth of the price, and Agency at $49 a month still costs about half of Promptwatch Essential while covering 20 brands.

Choose Promptwatch if you are a larger marketing team that wants the tool to produce content for you, needs hourly tracking, state and city level granularity, or shopping and ads insight on top of visibility monitoring.

## The bottom line

Promptwatch is a capable, feature-rich AI visibility platform, and its content agents and hourly tracking are genuinely useful for large teams. But at $95 a month for its cheapest plan, it costs five times BrandSight Pro for the same number of prompts. Unless you need the content production layer, BrandSight gives you the tracking that matters at a fraction of the price.
