## Botric pricing

Botric bundles AI visibility tracking with content generation and chat agents. Its pricing page lists a free plan and three paid plans:

- **Free: $0 per month.** 5 prompts, ChatGPT tracking, site and GEO audits, 800 lifetime credits, no card required.
- **Bit: $15 per month.** 15 prompts, ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, 1,500 credits per cycle, 1 chat agent, up to 6 blogs a month, 60-day logs.
- **Byte: $49 per month.** 25 prompts, adds Perplexity, 5,000 credits, up to 25 blogs a month, 2 seats, 90-day logs.
- **Infinity: $149 per month.** 150 prompts, adds Claude and Gemini, 15,000 credits, 5 workspaces, 5 sites, 5 seats, 5 chat agents, unlimited blogs, 120-day logs.

Annual billing lowers the paid plans to $12.50, $45, and $136 a month, and every paid plan starts with a 7-day free trial. A custom-priced Managed GEO service and Enterprise plan are also available.

Prices as listed on Botric pricing page, November 2026.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight focuses purely on AI visibility tracking: mentions, citations, and share of voice. Its pricing is simpler:

- **Free: $0 per month.** 1 brand, 5 prompts, weekly auto-checks, OpenAI and Gemini, and a weekly email report.
- **Pro: $19 per month.** 5 brands, 50 prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek, plus competitor share-of-voice alerts.
- **Agency: $49 per month.** 20 brands, 200 prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label reports, and CSV and JSON API export.

BrandSight prices verified live on brandsight-ai.coderao.com/pricing, November 2026.

## Head-to-head: what you get for the money

At entry level, Botric wins on price and BrandSight on prompt volume. Bit costs $15 a month for 15 prompts across 2 engines. Pro costs $19 a month for 50 prompts across 5 brands, with daily checks and DeepSeek included. For a handful of buyer questions, Bit is the cheaper start. For a real prompt list, BrandSight gives you more than three times the prompts for $4 more a month.

At the middle tier the gap widens. Botric Byte costs $49 a month for 25 prompts on 1 site, while BrandSight Agency costs the same $49 for 20 brands and 200 prompts. Botric Infinity at $149 a month buys 150 prompts across 5 sites.

The trade-off is scope. Botric is a wider product: it drafts AI-optimized blog posts, deploys chat agents on your site, and runs a weekly visibility audit on every plan. That bundle may justify the higher per-prompt cost. BrandSight does not write blogs or run chat agents. It tracks your brand across AI engines, with daily checks and competitor alerts on paid plans.

Engine coverage also differs. Botric tracks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini, with Google AI Mode, Grok, and DeepSeek on the roadmap, while BrandSight Pro already includes DeepSeek.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose Botric if you want tracking bundled with blog drafting and a chat agent, and your prompt list fits 15 or 25 prompts. Choose BrandSight if AI visibility is the job and you want more brands and prompts per dollar, with a free plan to start.

For a small business, the math is simple: $19 a month on BrandSight Pro buys 50 prompts across 5 brands with daily tracking, while $15 on Botric Bit buys 15 prompts across 2 engines. For an agency, BrandSight Agency at $49 covers 20 brands, while Botric Infinity costs $149 for 5 sites.

Either way, run a free check first and see which tool shows the gaps that cost you customers.
