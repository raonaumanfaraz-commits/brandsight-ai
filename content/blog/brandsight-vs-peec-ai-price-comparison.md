AI search is now a real channel: people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek for recommendations instead of scrolling through ten blue links. Tools that track how those models mention your brand are getting more expensive, and more confusing. This comparison puts two of them side by side on price first, then on what you get for the money.

Prices as listed on Peec AI pricing page, November 2026.

## What Peec AI costs

Peec AI is an AI search platform for marketing teams and SEO agencies. Its pricing page lists four tiers:

- **Starter, $95 per month:** 50 prompts, 3 active models, 1 project, daily tracking, unlimited users.
- **Pro, $245 per month:** 150 prompts, 3 active models, 2 projects, daily tracking, unlimited users.
- **Advanced, $495 per month:** 350 prompts, 3 active models, 5 projects, daily tracking, unlimited users.
- **Enterprise, custom:** fully customizable prompt tracking, unlimited projects, and up to 13 language models tracked.

All paid tiers include daily tracking, competitor tracking, citation share, source analytics, and shareable dashboards. Higher tiers add API access, a Data Studio connector, and more prompts fact-checked against facts you set for your brand. The models listed include ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, and Gemini, with Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, and Perplexity available on higher tiers.

## What BrandSight costs

BrandSight AI is a focused AI search visibility tracker. Its prices were confirmed live on its own pricing page in November 2026:

- **Free, $0 per month:** 1 brand, 5 prompts, weekly auto-checks, OpenAI and Google Gemini models, and a weekly email visibility report.
- **Pro, $19 per month:** 5 brands, 50 prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek, plus competitor share-of-voice alerts.
- **Agency, $49 per month:** 20 brands, 200 prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and client CSV and JSON API export.

There is no custom-quote tier and no per-seat charge listed on the page. The Pro plan costs one fifth of Peec AI's Starter plan, and the Agency plan costs half of Peec AI's Starter plan while covering 200 prompts across 20 brands.

## The price gap, explained honestly

The gap is not a trick. Peec AI is a broader platform: it tracks more models on higher tiers, adds prompt-volume and brand-perception scoring, crawlability audits, and agent workflows. A large team that needs all of that may genuinely use the Pro or Advanced tier.

BrandSight is narrower by design. It answers one question well: when someone asks an AI assistant about your market, does your brand get mentioned, in what position, and with which citations? If that is the question you need answered every day, paying $95 a month for a platform whose extra features you never open is hard to justify. The $76 a month saved by choosing BrandSight Pro over Peec AI Starter adds up to $912 over a year.

## Which one fits which buyer

Choose Peec AI if you are a larger team that needs many models, deep source analytics, crawl audits, and custom enterprise coverage, and your budget already covers a three-figure monthly tool.

Choose BrandSight if you are a founder, freelancer, or small agency that wants daily AI visibility tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek, competitor share of voice, and simple reports, without an enterprise-sized bill. The free tier is a sensible starting point: run your five prompts for a week, see whether your brand appears, and only then upgrade.

One fair warning applies to both tools: no tracker can force an AI model to mention you. Tracking shows you the gap. Closing it still takes clear product pages, factual content, and citations that models can find and trust.

## The bottom line

On price alone, BrandSight is the cheaper way to start tracking AI visibility, and its Agency tier undercuts even Peec AI's entry tier while covering more brands and prompts. Peec AI earns its higher price only if you use its broader platform features. For most small teams, the right move is to start small, measure for a month, and upgrade only when the data proves you need more.
