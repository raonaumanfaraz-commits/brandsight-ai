OtterlyAI and BrandSight AI both track how your brand appears in AI search answers, but they are priced for very different buyers. Otterly is a full AI visibility platform with audits, recommendations, and agency workspaces. BrandSight is a focused tracker that checks whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek mention your brand, where you appear in the answer, and which sources get cited. This comparison uses the prices listed on each company's own pricing page in November 2026.

## Otterly prices

Prices as listed on Otterly pricing page, November 2026. Otterly Lite costs $29 a month, or $25 a month billed annually, and includes 15 search prompts tracked daily across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot, with brand visibility and sentiment analysis, citation source tracking, and AI search optimization recommendations. Otterly Standard costs $189 a month, or $160 a month billed annually, and includes 100 search prompts, API and MCP access, and agent analytics. Otterly Premium costs $489 a month, or $422 a month billed annually, and includes 400 search prompts. Enterprise is custom priced and starts at 1,000 search prompts. Extra prompts cost $99 per 100 on Standard and Premium, and Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Mode are paid add-ons rather than included engines. Otterly offers a free trial for new users.

## BrandSight prices

BrandSight's prices, confirmed on its own pricing page in November 2026, are Free at $0 a month, Pro at $19 a month, and Agency at $49 a month. The free tier tracks 1 brand with 5 prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro tracks 5 brands with 50 prompts and daily auto-checks across OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek, and adds competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency tracks 20 brands with 200 prompts daily and adds white-label executive reports and CSV and JSON export.

## What the price gap buys

Otterly's cheapest paid plan costs more than BrandSight's Pro plan, and its Standard plan costs about ten times as much. For that money Otterly adds breadth: four AI engines in the base price, prompt research and query fan-out tools, a generative engine optimization audit, crawlability and content audits, and agency partner workspaces with pitch workspaces for prospects. BrandSight does not do content audits or recommendations. It does one thing: it runs your prompts through the AI models and reports mention rate, position, and citations.

The engine lists also differ in a way that matters for the price. Otterly's base plans track ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot, with Gemini and Claude as paid add-ons. BrandSight's Pro plan includes OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek in the $19 price, and its free tier already includes OpenAI and Gemini. If Gemini coverage matters to you, it is included at BrandSight's lowest paid tier and an add-on at Otterly.

## Which one fits your budget

If you are a mid-sized company or an agency that wants audits, recommendations, API access, and four engines under one roof, Otterly Standard or Premium is built for that job, and its price reflects it. If you are a solo founder, a small agency, or a SaaS startup that only needs to know whether AI assistants mention your brand and how often, BrandSight Pro covers the core job for $19 a month, and the free tier lets you check your visibility before paying anything.

Start with the free check. Run your brand through [BrandSight AI](https://brandsight-ai.coderao.com) and see whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek mention you at all. If the answer is never, that gap is worth fixing before you pay $189 a month for a bigger platform.
