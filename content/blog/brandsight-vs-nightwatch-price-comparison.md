AI search is now a real traffic channel, and both BrandSight and Nightwatch answer the same question: when someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation in your category, does your brand show up? Their pricing models work very differently. Nightwatch is a full search platform that bundles rank tracking with AI visibility; BrandSight is a focused AI visibility tracker.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simple with three flat monthly plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export.

There is no per-response metering and no charge per seat.

## Nightwatch pricing

Nightwatch charges by volume, not by seat: every plan includes the full toolkit and unlimited team seats. Its pricing page shows annual billing, with two months free on every plan.

Starter costs €79 a month billed yearly (€948 a year) and covers 500 keywords tracked daily, 50 AI prompts checked daily, 1,500 AI answers a month, 5 websites, and 5 competitors per site. Its AI platforms are ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews. Professional costs €159 a month billed yearly (€1,908 a year) and covers 2,500 keywords, 150 AI prompts, 4,500 AI answers a month, 25 websites, and 10 competitors per site, adding Looker Studio integration, white-label reports, and API access. Agency costs €399 a month billed yearly (€4,788 a year) and covers 7,500 keywords, 500 AI prompts, 15,000 AI answers a month, 100 websites, and 15 competitors per site, adding a dedicated account manager and priority support. Enterprise is custom priced. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Prices as listed on Nightwatch's pricing page, November 2026.

## Which one fits your budget?

The headline prices are about four times apart: $19 against €79. The real difference is what that money buys. Nightwatch's Starter gives you the same 50 prompts as BrandSight Pro, but it also buys 500 keywords of daily rank tracking, a site audit, keyword research, and a content studio, all on one platform. BrandSight's Pro checks 50 prompts daily across three engines for $19, which works out to roughly 4,500 AI responses a month against 1,500 on Nightwatch Starter.

Choose Nightwatch if you want one tool for rankings and AI visibility together, with your whole team on unlimited seats. Choose BrandSight if AI answers are the channel you care about and you want more prompts checked, more often, for less money.
