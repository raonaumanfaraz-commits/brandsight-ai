## What BrandSight Pro tracks

BrandSight focuses on one question: what do AI search engines say about your brand? Pro tracks 5 brands with 50 search prompts and daily auto-checks across ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek, and reports mentions, position, and citations, plus competitor share-of-voice alerts. The free tier tracks 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks. The Agency plan tracks 20 brands with 200 search prompts, adds daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, client CSV and JSON API export, and dedicated priority support. The difference from general monitoring tools is the focus: BrandSight measures visibility inside AI answers, not just mentions across the web.

## Who saves the most

The saving matters most for small agencies, software startups, and freelance consultants who track a handful of brands. At $19 a month, a year of BrandSight Pro costs $228, against $2,988 for a year of Brand24 Individual at $249 a month. An agency tracking client brands can start on the free plan, move to Pro for daily checks, and step up to the $49 Agency plan only when the brand count grows.
