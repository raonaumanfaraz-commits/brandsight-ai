AI search is now a real traffic channel, and both BrandSight and Measure answer the same question: when someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation in your category, does your brand show up? Their pricing models work very differently, and that difference matters for your budget.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simple with three flat monthly plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export.

There is no per-prompt metering: what you see is what you pay.

## Measure pricing

Measure is a self-serve AI visibility tracker with a free plan and four paid tiers. Free costs $0 a month and covers 3 prompts tracked weekly on Perplexity and ChatGPT, with GA4 and Search Console attribution included. One catch: a card is required to start the free plan, though Measure says it is never charged unless you upgrade.

Basic costs $19.99 a month, or $14.99 a month billed yearly, and covers 10 tracked prompts. Starter costs $89 a month, or $49 a month billed yearly, and covers 25 tracked prompts. Pro costs $199 a month, or $129 a month billed yearly, and covers 100 tracked prompts. Advanced costs $499 a month, or $379 a month billed yearly, and covers 300 tracked prompts. Enterprise is custom priced.

On paid plans, Perplexity is scanned daily while ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude are scanned weekly. Google AI Overviews is available only through on-demand live runs, not scheduled scans.

Prices as listed on Measure's features page, November 2026.

## Which one fits your budget?

The headline prices look almost identical: $19 against $19.99. The real difference is what that money buys: BrandSight Pro gives you 50 prompts, checked daily, for $0.38 each, while Measure Basic gives you 10 prompts for $2.00 each, with ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude checked weekly.

Measure earns its price in two places: GA4 and Search Console attribution is built in on every plan, including Free, so you can show whether AI mentions actually send visitors; and it checks every answer against your stored brand facts, flagging a wrong price or date with the quote and the truth. If proving traffic value to a client or a manager is the job, those two features are genuinely useful.

BrandSight is the cheaper way to track more questions more often: its Agency plan at $49 still costs less than Measure Starter at $89, while covering 20 brands with 200 prompts checked daily, and it tracks DeepSeek, which Measure does not list among its scheduled engines.

## The bottom line

On price alone, BrandSight is the cheaper way to start: more prompts, daily checks, and an Agency tier that undercuts Measure's second tier. Measure earns its higher per-prompt price only if you need GA4 attribution and answer-accuracy checks to prove what AI visibility is worth. For most small teams, the right move is to start small and upgrade only when the data proves you need more.
