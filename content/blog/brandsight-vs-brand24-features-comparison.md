Brand24 and BrandSight AI both answer a version of the same question: what is the internet saying about your brand? Brand24 listens to social media, news, blogs, forums, and podcasts and counts mentions, while BrandSight AI asks ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek about your brand and records whether you are mentioned, where you appear, and which sources get cited.

That difference matters more than any feature list. If your buyers ask an AI assistant before they ever run a Google search, you need to know what the assistant says. This comparison looks at what each tool actually does, so you can pick the one that matches the job.

## What each tool is built to do

Brand24 is a social listening platform. Its core job is collecting mentions of your keywords across Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, news sites, blogs, reviews, newsletters, and podcasts, then showing volume, reach, sentiment, and the most influential authors. AI features sit on top of that listening: AI sentiment analysis, event detection that explains sudden spikes, an AI brand assistant, AI insights, and AI topics.

BrandSight AI is narrower by design. It does not track social mentions. It runs a fixed set of prompts about your brand and your category through OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek, then records mention rate, position in the answer, and the sources cited. Pro adds competitor share-of-voice alerts, and the free tier includes a weekly email visibility report.

## AI visibility features, side by side

Brand24 sells AI visibility as a module. Its pricing page lists the AI Visibility module as an add-on on every plan, including Enterprise, and describes it as tracking your brand's exposure across major AI platforms, showing where, how often, and how positively your brand is represented. No separate price for the module is shown on the pricing page, so the total cost is the plan price plus an add-on you have to ask about.

BrandSight includes AI visibility in every plan, including the free one. The free tier runs 5 prompts for 1 brand weekly. Pro, at $19 a month, runs 50 prompts for 5 brands daily across all three models. Agency, at $49 a month, runs 200 prompts for 20 brands daily and adds white-label executive reports and CSV and JSON export. No add-on is needed before you can see an AI answer about your brand.

## Price comparison

Prices as listed on Brand24 pricing page, November 2026. Brand24 Individual costs $249 a month, or $199 a month billed annually. Team costs $349 a month, or $299 billed annually. Pro costs $499 a month, or $399 billed annually, with 12 keywords and 40,000 mentions a month. Business costs $699 a month, or $599 billed annually, with 25 keywords and 100,000 mentions a month. Enterprise starts at $1,499 a month billed annually with a custom keyword count. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, and AI visibility is an add-on on all of them.

BrandSight's prices, confirmed on its own pricing page in November 2026, are Free at $0 a month, Pro at $19 a month, and Agency at $49 a month. The cheapest Brand24 plan costs more than ten times the BrandSight Pro plan, before the AI visibility add-on.

## Which one fits your job

If you need one platform that watches social mentions, tracks reach and influencers, and also reports on AI visibility, Brand24 is the broader tool. If your question is only what AI assistants say about your brand, and you want that answer daily for a small budget, BrandSight covers the job directly, with no add-on and no enterprise sales call. A small agency tracking five client brands can run the whole routine on BrandSight Pro for $19 a month; the same routine on Brand24 starts at $249 a month plus the add-on.

Start with the free check. Run your brand through [BrandSight AI](https://brandsight-ai.coderao.com) and see whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek mention you at all before you pay for a listening platform. If the answer is never, that is the gap to fix first.
