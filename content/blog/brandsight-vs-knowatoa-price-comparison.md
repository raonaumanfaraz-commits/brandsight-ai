AI search is now a real traffic channel, and both BrandSight and Knowatoa exist to answer the same question: when someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation in your category, does your brand show up? The tools are similar in purpose, but their pricing models work very differently, and that difference matters for your budget.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simple with three flat monthly plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export.

There is no per-prompt metering and no usage-based billing on any plan. What you see is what you pay, and the free tier is genuinely free, not a time-limited trial.

## Knowatoa pricing

Knowatoa is an AI search visibility tracker with a guided audit approach: it builds the question set from your site and market, then checks how AI assistants answer those questions. Its pricing page lists three tiers.

Starter costs $59 a month and covers three AI search services: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode. It includes competitor visibility gaps, citation tracking, brand sentiment alerts, and a weekly digest of what changed. Growth costs $199 a month and covers all seven AI search services, adding Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Meta AI, plus AI agents and the full reporting stack of API, MCP, Looker Studio, and NinjaCat. Enterprise is custom priced and adds a dedicated account representative, custom volume, custom integrations, and priority feature requests. Both self-serve plans offer a free trial, and annual pricing is available.

One detail worth knowing before you buy: the pricing page does not publish a numeric question limit, so check the plan screen for the current limit before you commit.

## Which one fits your budget?

The biggest difference is the entry price. BrandSight Pro costs $19 a month, while Knowatoa Starter costs $59. That is $40 a month saved, or $480 over a year, and BrandSight's Agency plan at $49 still costs less than Knowatoa's entry tier while covering 20 brands with 200 prompts.

Knowatoa earns its higher price if you need its wider engine coverage and guided audits. Growth at $199 covers seven services, flags answers that are wrong, outdated, or favour a competitor, with fix recommendations attached to each audit. A team that needs Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity tracking in one place, plus API and Looker Studio reporting, may genuinely use that tier.

BrandSight is narrower by design. It answers one question well: when someone asks an AI assistant about your market, does your brand get mentioned, in what position, and with which citations? If that is the question you need answered every day, paying $59 a month for a platform whose extra features you never open is hard to justify.

## The bottom line

On price alone, BrandSight is the cheaper way to start tracking AI visibility, and its Agency tier undercuts even Knowatoa's entry tier while covering more brands and prompts. Knowatoa earns its higher price only if you use its seven-engine coverage, guided audits, and reporting stack. For most small teams, the right move is to start small, measure for a month, and upgrade only when the data proves you need more.
