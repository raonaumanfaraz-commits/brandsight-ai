Brand monitoring and AI visibility tracking sound similar, but they are different jobs with very different price tags. Mentionlytics is a social listening tool that tracks mentions across the web and social media, with AI visibility sold as an add-on. BrandSight AI is built only for AI search visibility: it checks how ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek mention your brand. This comparison sticks to the published prices on each company's own pricing page.

## The prices, side by side

Mentionlytics lists five listening plans on its pricing page. Basic costs $69 a month and covers 3 keyword rules, 5,000 monthly mentions, updates every 12 hours, 10 social profiles, and 2 users. Essential costs $169 a month and covers 10 keyword rules, 15,000 monthly mentions, updates every 6 hours, 20 social profiles, and 10 users. Advanced costs $299 a month and covers 15 keyword rules, 50,000 monthly mentions, continuous updates, 30 social profiles, and unlimited users. Pro costs $499 a month and covers 25 keyword rules, 100,000 monthly mentions, continuous updates, and 40 social profiles. Business and Enterprise plans are priced on request through a demo booking.

BrandSight's pricing is simpler. The Free tier costs $0 a month and tracks 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks. Pro costs $19 a month and tracks 5 brands with 50 search prompts and daily auto-checks. The Agency plan costs $49 a month and tracks 20 brands with 200 search prompts and daily auto-checks.

*Prices as listed on Mentionlytics pricing page, November 2026. BrandSight prices as listed on brandsight-ai.coderao.com, November 2026.*

## What the AI Visibility add-on costs

This is the part that matters most if your goal is AI search visibility rather than social listening. Mentionlytics does not include AI visibility in its listening plans. It is a credits-based add-on, listed from $49 a month, and it requires an Essential plan or higher. The add-on's Pro tier lists $169 a month for 100 prompts, its Business tier lists $379 a month for 250 prompts, and its Enterprise tier lists $559 a month for 400 or more prompts.

That means the lowest published route to AI visibility tracking through Mentionlytics pairs the Essential listening plan at $169 a month with an AI Visibility tier, which starts from $49 a month. By contrast, BrandSight Pro includes AI visibility tracking for 5 brands and 50 prompts at $19 a month, with no separate listening plan required. The two products are not like-for-like: Mentionlytics bundles broad social and web listening with an AI add-on, while BrandSight covers only AI search visibility.

## Which budget each tool fits

If you need full social listening, review monitoring, and web mentions across many profiles, Mentionlytics' listening plans cover that breadth, and the price reflects it. Its Basic plan at $69 a month suits a small brand that wants to watch a few keywords across social and web sources.

If your question is narrower, namely what AI assistants say about your brand, when they recommend it, and which sources they cite, a dedicated AI visibility tracker costs far less. BrandSight's Free tier answers that question for one brand, and Pro at $19 a month extends it to five brands with daily checks and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agencies managing many clients can use the Agency plan at $49 a month for 20 brands, 200 prompts, and white-label reports.

The honest way to choose is to match the tool to the question. Pay for broad listening when you need to hear everything said about you online. Pay for a focused AI visibility tracker when you need to know how AI answers talk about you. For that second job, the price gap is large: $19 a month against a listening plan plus an add-on that starts well above it.
