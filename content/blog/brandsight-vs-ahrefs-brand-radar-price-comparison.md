If you want to know how ChatGPT and other AI tools talk about your brand, Ahrefs Brand Radar and BrandSight both offer a way in. The difference is what you are actually buying. Brand Radar is one feature inside the Ahrefs universe, sold either bundled with a full SEO suite or as a standalone prompt package. BrandSight is a dedicated AI visibility tracker with nothing else attached. The price gap is wide, so it is worth seeing what each option covers.

## Ahrefs Brand Radar pricing

Ahrefs sells Brand Radar AI as a standalone tool with custom prompt packages. Basic costs $50 a month and adds 2,500 checks a month, enough for about 80 prompts a day on one platform, with overage at $0.020 per check. Growth costs $100 a month and adds 7,000 checks a month, about 100 prompts a day across two platforms, with overage at $0.015 per check. Scale costs $250 a month and adds 25,000 checks a month at the lowest per-check rate, with overage at $0.010 per check.

Brand Radar is also included in Ahrefs main plans, where tracked AI prompts range from 150 checks a month on the entry plan to 600 on the higher plan, with 5 to 20 prompts. The tool draws on a database of more than 542 million organic prompts and lets you research any brand, not just your own. It is a strong research product, but it is priced like an add-on to a much larger SEO platform, and its standalone packages are built for teams that already live inside Ahrefs.

Prices as listed on Ahrefs pricing page, November 2026.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight is priced for AI visibility alone. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks. Pro costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts and daily auto-checks, across OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek. Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts and daily auto-checks, plus white-label reports and API export.

Prices as listed on BrandSight pricing page, November 2026.

## Which one fits your budget

The math is simple. Brand Radar Basic at $50 a month buys 2,500 prompt checks, which suits a team that wants deep research on a modest prompt set and already uses Ahrefs for SEO. BrandSight Pro at $19 a month buys 50 prompts checked daily, which is 1,500 checks a month, for less than half the price of the cheapest Brand Radar package. For a SaaS startup or a small agency tracking a handful of brands, BrandSight covers the daily monitoring job at a fraction of the cost.

Where Brand Radar earns its price is breadth. If you need to research any brand in the market, explore hundreds of millions of real prompts, and pair AI visibility with backlink and keyword data, Ahrefs is the deeper tool. If your goal is narrower, to know every day whether AI answers mention your brand, cite your site, and recommend you over competitors, a focused tracker does the job without the SEO suite attached.

Ahrefs Brand Radar is a capable AI visibility product inside a premium SEO platform, with standalone packages from $50 to $250 a month.
