AI answer engines now shape buying decisions, so tracking whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity mention your brand has become a real budget line. BrandSight and Citlyze are two purpose-built AI visibility trackers, and their entry prices sit close together. This comparison breaks down what each dollar actually buys.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight has three plans. The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 prompts and weekly checks. The Pro plan costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands, 50 prompts and daily checks across OpenAI, Gemini and DeepSeek, with competitor share-of-voice alerts. The Agency plan costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands, 200 prompts and daily checks, with white-label reports and API export.

Prices as listed on the BrandSight pricing page, November 2026.

## Citlyze pricing

Citlyze also has a free trial, 14 days with no credit card required. Its paid plans start higher. The Starter plan costs $29 a month and tracks 12 prompts a day for one brand, with 1 seat, 50 audit credits a month and 60 days of history. The Growth plan costs $99 a month and tracks 40 prompts a day, with 3 seats, 300 audit credits and 12 months of history. The Pro plan costs $249 a month and tracks 80 prompts a day, with 5 seats, 1,000 audit credits and 24 months of history. A Custom enterprise plan is available on request.

Prices as listed on Citlyze pricing page, November 2026.

## Engines covered

Both tools track the four core engines: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and DeepSeek. Citlyze adds Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Bing Copilot on every plan, checked weekly, and Grok on its Pro plan. Claude is a paid add-on on every Citlyze plan at $29 a month for up to 25 prompts a day. BrandSight's Pro and Agency plans cover OpenAI, Gemini and DeepSeek with daily checks.

## Prompts per dollar

This is where the gap opens up. At the entry level, Citlyze Starter gives 12 prompts a day for $29 a month. BrandSight Pro gives 50 prompts for $19 a month, checked daily. A small business tracking a handful of buyer questions will usually find BrandSight's prompt allowance more generous at the lower price. Citlyze's strength is measurement depth: it runs each prompt repeatedly per window and shows the run count next to every rate, so a mention rate reflects a distribution of answers rather than a single check.

## Seats and reporting

Citlyze Starter includes just 1 seat, while Growth adds 3 seats and Pro adds 5. BrandSight's Agency plan at $49 a month is aimed at agencies managing client brands, with white-label executive reports and CSV and JSON export. Citlyze reserves PDF export and scheduled reports for its higher plans, and API access is a $49 a month add-on. If you need to hand a branded report to a client, compare the total cost carefully: Citlyze Pro at $249 a month is a very different commitment from BrandSight Agency at $49 a month.

## Which one fits your budget

Choose Citlyze if you want repeated-run sampling, audit credits and crawler analytics, and you can use its Growth or Pro tiers. Choose BrandSight if you want daily tracking across several brands at a lower monthly price, with more prompts per dollar and simple agency reporting. A solo founder or small SaaS team tracking up to 50 prompts will usually get further with BrandSight Pro at $19 a month. A team that needs deep sampling and audit workflows may find Citlyze's $99 Growth plan worth the extra cost.
