GELIOS is the newest name in AI visibility tracking. Launched in October 2026 by the Promodo SEO agency, it checks whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews recommend your brand. BrandSight answers the same question with a flat three-plan model. Here is how the two compare on price, prompts, and what is included.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight has three flat monthly plans. Free costs $0 a month: 1 brand, 5 search prompts, weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. Pro costs $19 a month: 5 brands, 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek tracking, and competitor share-of-voice alerts. Agency costs $49 a month: 20 brands, 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and CSV and JSON API export. There is no per-response metering and no charge per seat.

## GELIOS pricing

GELIOS charges per plan, with prompts shared across all brands on that plan. Every plan includes ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, a visibility dashboard, a six-month trend, unlimited team members, and full data history. Every plan checks daily and allows up to 10 competitors per brand. The first check is free, and paid plans start with a 7-day free trial.

Standard costs $29 a month, or $23 a month billed annually, and covers 20 prompts across 1 brand. Pro costs $99 a month, or $79 a month billed annually, and covers 100 prompts across up to 3 brands. Agency costs $249 a month, or $199 a month billed annually, and covers 300 prompts across up to 5 brands. Custom starts at $286 a month billed yearly. Prices exclude tax. To unlock the full report, GELIOS asks for a card, and the bank places a temporary $1 hold that is released right away.

Prices as listed on GELIOS pricing page, November 2026.

## What the price gap buys

The headline gap is at the entry level: $19 a month for BrandSight Pro against $29 a month for GELIOS Standard, and BrandSight gives you 50 prompts across 5 brands where GELIOS gives 20 prompts for 1 brand. That makes BrandSight the cheaper way to track several brands at once.

GELIOS earns its higher entry price in two places. First, it includes Google AI Overviews on every plan, so you see your brand in live Google search results as well as in chatbot answers. Second, it bundles unlimited team members and a six-month trend on every plan, with no seat charges. Its prompts are also chosen automatically from your website.

## Which one fits

Pick GELIOS if Google AI Overviews is a channel you need to watch, if you want prompts picked for you from your website, or if a large team needs access with no per-seat cost. The 7-day free trial is the way to test whether its three-engine view matches how your customers actually search.

Pick BrandSight if you want more prompts and more brands for less money. Pro at $19 a month covers five brands with 50 prompts and daily checks across ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek, and the free tier covers a single brand with weekly checks at no cost. For an agency, the Agency plan at $49 a month covers 20 brands with 200 prompts, which is less than a single GELIOS Agency plan.
