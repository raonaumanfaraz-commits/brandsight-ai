AI visibility tools are multiplying, and AIclicks is one of the newer names in the space, pairing daily brand tracking with AI-written articles. BrandSight does the tracking job at a much lower price. Here is how the two compare, plan by plan.

## AIclicks pricing

AIclicks sells three self-serve plans and a custom Enterprise plan. Every paid plan tracks prompts daily, includes unlimited team seats, and starts with a 3-day free trial that needs a card:

- **Starter: $59 per month.** 30 tracked prompts, 3 AI engines of your choice, 1 website, 1 country per project, 2,790 LLM responses a month, 5 AI-optimized articles a month, CMS integration, email support.
- **Pro: $189 per month.** 150 tracked prompts, 4 AI engines, unlimited websites and countries, 18,600 LLM responses a month, 15 AI-optimized articles a month, GA4, API and MCP access, Slack support.
- **Business: $499 per month.** 300 tracked prompts, 6 AI engines, unlimited websites and countries, 55,800 LLM responses a month, 30 AI-optimized articles a month, dedicated account specialist, live demo with the team.
- **Enterprise: custom price.** All AI models, custom prompt volume, custom integrations, dedicated account specialist.

Annual billing lowers the three paid plans to $49, $140, and $357 a month. The engine menu covers more than 10 platforms, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Grok, DeepSeek, and Meta AI, but each plan only tracks a fixed number of them. Full coverage of every engine is Enterprise only.

Prices as listed on AIclicks pricing page, November 2026.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight focuses purely on AI visibility tracking: mentions, citations, and share of voice. Its pricing is simpler:

- **Free: $0 per month.** 1 brand, 5 prompts, weekly auto-checks, OpenAI and Gemini, and a weekly email report.
- **Pro: $19 per month.** 5 brands, 50 prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek, competitor share-of-voice alerts.
- **Agency: $49 per month.** 20 brands, 200 prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, client CSV and JSON API export, dedicated priority support.

Prices as listed on BrandSight pricing page, November 2026.

## Which one fits your budget?

The gap is stark at the entry level. AIclicks Starter costs $59 a month for 30 prompts on 3 engines and one website. BrandSight Pro costs $19 a month for 50 prompts across 5 brands, with daily checks. For a small business or solo founder tracking one brand, BrandSight Pro covers more prompts for about a third of the price.

AIclicks earns its higher price in a different way: every plan generates AI-optimized articles aimed at the visibility gaps it finds, from 5 a month on Starter to 30 on Business. If you want tracking plus content generation in one subscription, and you are willing to pay $59 or more a month for it, that bundle is the reason to pick AIclicks. BrandSight does not generate articles; it tracks and reports, and leaves the content to you.

Scale the comparison up and the pattern holds. AIclicks Pro at $189 a month buys 150 prompts, 4 engines, and API access. BrandSight Agency at $49 a month buys 200 prompts across 20 brands with white-label reports. An agency managing several client brands gets more tracking capacity from BrandSight Agency at roughly a quarter of the price, though it gives up the article generation and the API.

## The bottom line

Choose AIclicks if you want one tool that both tracks AI visibility and writes the articles to improve it, and your budget starts at $59 a month. Choose [BrandSight AI](https://brandsight-ai.coderao.com) if tracking is the job and you want the most prompts per dollar: $19 a month for 50 daily prompts across 5 brands, or $49 a month for 200 prompts across 20 client brands. For most small businesses and agencies, the tracking-first option is the cheaper way to answer the question that matters: are AI answers mentioning your brand, or your competitor?
