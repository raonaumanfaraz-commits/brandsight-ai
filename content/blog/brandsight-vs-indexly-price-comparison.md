## Indexly pricing

Indexly sells AI visibility tracking bundled with SEO audits and indexing tools. Its pricing page lists two paid plans:

- **Starter: $49 per month.** 1 site, 50 prompts tracked, any 3 AI models, 1 country, brand sentiment, citation gaps, fact checker, competitor analysis, and AI shopping.
- **Growth: $99 per month.** 2 sites, 100 prompts tracked daily, any 3 AI models, 2 countries, and the same feature set.
- **Agency and custom plans:** priced on request, for teams managing several client brands.

Indexly offers a 17% discount for annual billing, and a $1 first-month offer is shown on the Starter and Growth plans.

Prices as listed on Indexly pricing page, November 2026.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight focuses purely on AI visibility tracking: mentions, citations, and share of voice across AI engines. Its pricing is simpler:

- **Free: $0 per month.** 1 brand, 5 search prompts, weekly auto-checks, OpenAI and Google Gemini models, and a weekly email visibility report.
- **Pro: $19 per month.** 5 brands, 50 search prompts, daily auto-checks, OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek, plus competitor share-of-voice alerts.
- **Agency: $49 per month.** 20 brands, 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, and client CSV and JSON API export.

BrandSight prices verified live on brandsight-ai.coderao.com/pricing, November 2026.

## Head-to-head: what you get for the money

The entry-level comparison is stark. Indexly Starter costs $49 a month for 1 site and 50 prompts. BrandSight Pro costs $19 a month for 5 brands and the same 50 prompts, with daily checks included. At Indexly's Growth tier of $99 a month, you get 2 sites and 100 prompts; BrandSight Agency costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 prompts.

The trade-off is scope. Indexly bundles traditional SEO audits, crawlability checks, backlink monitoring, and an automatic llms.txt generator alongside its AI visibility tracking. If you want one tool that covers both classic SEO health and AI search, that bundle may justify the higher price. BrandSight does not do SEO audits. It does one thing: track your brand across AI engines, with daily checks and competitor alerts on paid plans.

Indexly also limits you to 3 AI models of your choice per plan, while BrandSight Pro includes OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek as standard. And Indexly's free tier does not exist as a listed plan, while BrandSight lets you track one brand for free, weekly, before paying anything.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose Indexly if you need SEO audits and AI visibility in a single subscription and you are comfortable paying from $49 a month for one site. Choose BrandSight if AI visibility is the job and you want more brands and prompts per dollar, with a genuinely free plan to start.

For a small business or a solo founder, the math is simple: $19 a month on BrandSight Pro buys the same 50 prompts as $49 a month on Indexly Starter, plus four extra brands and daily tracking. For an agency, BrandSight Agency at $49 a month covers 20 brands, while Indexly's agency pricing is custom and its listed Growth plan covers only 2 sites for $99 a month.

Either way, the right test is the same: run a free check, look at the prompts that matter to your buyers, and see which tool shows you the gaps that actually cost you customers.
