LLMrefs and BrandSight both track what AI search engines say about your brand, but they price that tracking in very different ways. LLMrefs sells one all-in plan at $79 a month. BrandSight sells three flat tiers, starting free and topping out at $49 a month. The right choice depends on how many prompts you need and how many brands you manage.

## LLMrefs pricing

LLMrefs has a single plan, called All in One, aimed at marketing teams. Prices as listed on LLMrefs pricing page, November 2026.

The All in One plan costs $79 per month. It includes 500 prompts for brand mentions, sources and fan-out queries, access to all AI search engines with no extra fees, weekly AI visibility reports, citation tracking, geo-targeting in more than 50 countries and 20 languages, unlimited team members, unlimited projects for all your domains, monthly prompt volume estimates, CSV export and API access. A 7-day free trial is offered, with no credit card required to start.

One thing that sets LLMrefs apart is its approach: it tracks keywords rather than individual prompts, then auto-generates fan-out prompts from real conversations people have with AI chatbots. It covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Grok, Copilot, Meta AI and DeepSeek.

## BrandSight pricing

BrandSight keeps it simpler, with three flat plans. Prices verified live on the BrandSight pricing page, November 2026.

The Free tier costs $0 a month and covers 1 brand with 5 search prompts and weekly auto-checks across OpenAI and Google Gemini, plus a weekly email visibility report. The Pro plan costs $19 a month and covers 5 brands with 50 search prompts and daily auto-checks across OpenAI, Gemini and DeepSeek, plus competitor share-of-voice alerts. The Agency plan costs $49 a month and covers 20 brands with 200 search prompts, daily auto-checks, white-label executive reports, CSV and JSON API export, and priority support.

## What $79 buys versus $19

The headline gap is large: LLMrefs costs four times BrandSight Pro. But the prompt allowance is ten times bigger, at 500 prompts against 50. Per prompt, LLMrefs works out cheaper, at about 16 cents per prompt a month against about 38 cents for BrandSight Pro.

Volume is not the whole story. BrandSight Pro tracks five brands for $19 a month, while LLMrefs covers unlimited projects under one subscription, so an agency with many client domains may find the single LLMrefs plan simpler than counting brands. On the other hand, a solo founder tracking one or two brands pays $79 a month at LLMrefs for capacity they may never use, when BrandSight Pro covers the same job for $19.

There is also a free starting point to consider. BrandSight offers a genuinely free tier with weekly checks, so you can see your brand's AI visibility before spending anything. LLMrefs offers a 7-day free trial instead, which is generous but time-limited.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose LLMrefs if you track hundreds of keywords, need geo-targeting across many countries, or run an agency where unlimited projects and seats under one subscription matter more than the monthly bill. Choose BrandSight if you want a low fixed cost, track a handful of brands, or want to start free and upgrade only when daily checks and competitor alerts become worth $19 a month.

Either way, the real test is the same: run both on your own brand for a week and see which dashboard answers your questions faster. Start with a free check at [BrandSight AI](https://brandsight-ai.coderao.com) and compare the results against whatever you track today.
